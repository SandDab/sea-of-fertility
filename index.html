豊饒の海

The Sea of Fertility

Yukio Mishima's final tetralogy follows a single soul across four incarnations, from the flower of Meiji aristocracy through the violence of nationalist Japan, the decadence of postwar Asia, and into the silence beyond. This companion maps the world the novels move through — its places, its images, the art and literature they contain.
